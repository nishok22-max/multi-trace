## 1. Verdict: **PASS-WITH-FIXES**

The plan is unusually well grounded. It has a hexagonal core, fakes first, named tests per task, a lint-enforced never-average rule, `.gitattributes` for seed bytes, replay-only OCR on Windows and a CSP spike at h1. I found no architectural defect that needs a redesign.

I did find three **BLOCKERs** that must be fixed before M3 starts:
1. **The plan has no task or test for T-39.** It also has a likely path for leaking secrets into the image.
2. **Untrusted PDF parsing can happen in the API process**, outside the sandbox. This happens through page counting at upload and page rendering when the PNG cache misses.
3. **The upload cap as designed runs after Starlette has already spooled the whole body.** With a tmpfs `/tmp` on a read-only rootfs, that spool sits in RAM.

All three fixes fit in A's 1.5 h buffer plus about 1 h taken from D's reserve.

I also found 15 **MAJOR** issues, mostly in two areas:
- latency and locking on the decision path;
- pilot operations: egress, OIDC attribution, upgrades and retention.

These must be scheduled into the named phases before the phase exit. MINOR items can be handled during normal work.

**Counts:** 3 BLOCKER · 15 MAJOR · 14 MINOR (32 findings).
